description: Experimental no-op overlay for keeping model summaries out of durable RemainOn knowledge.
---

Read and follow the production skill at `../../../../SKILL.md`, then apply only
this experimental addition.

A model-generated summary is intake material, not verified evidence and not a
durable source. Do not promote its claims or numbers into rules, memory, policy,
reports presented as fact, or production knowledge. Return `no_op` or `blocked`
when asked to do so.

Any future citation must point to the original source URL and an exact timestamp
that directly supports the claim. The summary itself is never the citation.
